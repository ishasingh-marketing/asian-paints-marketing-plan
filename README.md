# Asian Paints Ltd. – Strategic Marketing Plan

## Project Overview
An individual academic project developed as part of the Advanced Marketing Management course at the Fortune Institute of International Business (FIIB), New Delhi.

This project develops a strategic marketing plan for Asian Paints Ltd., examining its competitive position in India's paints and home décor industry and identifying opportunities for growth through sustainability, customer experience, and marketing technology.

## Project Objectives
- Analyse the company's internal strengths and weaknesses and external market environment.
- Evaluate industry trends, competition, consumer behaviour, and growth opportunities.
- Develop a marketing strategy using the 7Ps marketing mix.
- Propose initiatives to strengthen customer engagement, sustainability, and market expansion.
- Define marketing budgets, KPIs, and an implementation roadmap.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Company and Market Analysis
An overview of Asian Paints' business, brand positioning, distribution network, and competitive landscape.

### 2. SWOT Analysis
Assessment of the company's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, including market leadership, competitive pressures, raw-material costs, and sustainability opportunities.

### 3. PESTEL Analysis
Evaluation of political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal factors influencing the paints and home décor industry.

### 4. Marketing Strategy – 7Ps
Strategic recommendations across Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, and Physical Evidence.

### 5. Sustainability and Product Innovation
A proposed Royale Eco+ product line focused on sustainable, low-VOC interior paints and professional design consultations.

### 6. AI-Enabled Marketing Concept
**Paint Re-Alert & Contractor Connect:** A proposed concept for predicting repainting cycles, sending personalised homeowner reminders, connecting customers with authorised contractors, and supporting repeat purchases.

### 7. Budget, KPIs, and Implementation
A proposed marketing budget, performance indicators, and phased implementation roadmap for the recommended initiatives.

## Key Learning Outcomes
- Application of strategic marketing frameworks to a real-world brand.
- Understanding of competitive positioning and market dynamics.
- Development of integrated marketing strategies.
- Linking customer experience and sustainability with brand differentiation.
- Exploring AI-enabled customer engagement and retention opportunities.
